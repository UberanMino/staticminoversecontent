# Painting sources

Public-domain artworks from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access program
(`isPublicDomain: true` via https://metmuseum.github.io/). These are used as the
classical-painting backplates for the "old painting head-swap" approach.

| File | Work | Artist | Met object |
|------|------|--------|-----------|
| `vangogh_strawhat.jpg` | Self-Portrait with a Straw Hat | Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) | 436532 |
| `poussin_sabine.jpg` | The Abduction of the Sabine Women | Nicolas Poussin (1594–1665) | 437329 |

Both artists died well over 100 years ago; the works are in the public domain.
Drop any other old painting here and reference it from a recipe.
